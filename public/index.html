The Problem

Romblon isn't one destination. It's a chain of them.

Getting from Tablas to Sibuyan, or Romblon, means working around ferry schedules that don't run on your itinerary's clock. Most trip planners are built for cities with roads — not provinces held together by boats.

Adventure is built the other way around: routes, terminals, and ferries come first, and your itinerary is built to fit them.
