Summary

About Adventure

Adventure is a mobile travel companion built specifically for Romblon Province, Philippines. It plans multi-island itineraries around real ferry routes and terminals, works offline for weak inter-island signal, and includes Isla, an in-app AI trip assistant. Beyond trip planning, the app features a province-wide events calendar for local fiestas and festivals, a digital travel journal and photo memories album. Adventure is developed independently by Bryan Ronnie Briones, in coordination with local organizations across Romblon, with a long-term vision of expanding province by province toward a unified Philippine tourism and inter-island ferry navigation platform.

Founder

Bryan Ronnie Briones

Bryan is the sole developer behind Adventure. He has a background in Information Systems and a long-standing personal interest in Romblon Province tourism, and has worked on the app's architecture, design, and content — including coordinating with local organizations across the province — independently.

He is available for interviews and press inquiries via the contact page.
