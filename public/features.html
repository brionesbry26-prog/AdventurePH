Features

Everything between "I want to go" and "I'm there."

Adventure handles the parts of island travel that generic trip planners weren't built for — ferries, terminals, and getting there in the right order.

Trip planning

Routes built around real ferry connections.

Tell Adventure where you're starting and where you want to end up — one municipality, or a full multi-island chain like Tablas → Romblon → Sibuyan. Your itinerary is generated day-by-day, with places already grouped by what's actually reachable between ferry crossings.

  • Multi-island routing
  • Day-by-day itinerary
  • Drag to reorder
  • Add or remove days anytime

Ferries & terminals

Know the crossing before you're at the dock.

Every terminal in your route — with the ferries that connect it — is built into your itinerary as a first-class stop, not an afterthought. See connection points, book a ferry within your plan, and get a reminder before departure day.

  • Terminal-to-terminal routing
  • In-app ferry booking reminders
  • Multi-leg route chains

Isla, your trip assistant

An assistant that already knows your itinerary.

Isla isn't a generic chatbot bolted onto the app — she's scoped to the trip you're actually on. Ask what's near your Day 3 stop, whether a fiesta lines up with your dates, or what to do with a gap in the schedule.

  • Itinerary-aware answers
  • Local knowledge, municipality by municipality
  • Proactive suggestions as your trip nears

Offline-ready

Your plan doesn't need signal to work.

Inter-island crossings are exactly where signal disappears — so your saved itinerary, maps, and terminal details are cached on your device ahead of time. Changes sync automatically the moment you're back online.

  • Offline itinerary access
  • Cached maps & terminal info
  • Automatic background sync

Memories

A travel journal you didn't have to build.

Mark a place visited, and Adventure opens the door to drop in a photo and a quick note right there. By the end of the trip, every stop has a memory attached — organized by place, automatically.

  • Photo memories per stop
  • Automatic trip timeline
  • Private by default

Group trips

One itinerary, everyone on the same page.

Share your trip by QR code — anyone who scans in sees the same route, the same days, and the same ferry booking. Visited places sync across the whole group, so no one's asking "wait, did we already go there?"

  • QR code trip joining
  • Shared visited-place tracking
  • Group photo memories
