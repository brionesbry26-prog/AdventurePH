About

Built for the islands people keep overlooking.

Adventure isn't a travel-tech startup with a Romblon feature. It started the other way around — a personal interest in the province that turned into an app.

The Story

Why Romblon, specifically.

Adventure is built by Bryan Ronnie Briones, who has a background in Information Systems and a personal, long-standing interest in Romblon Province tourism. The app started as an independent project — not a client brief — driven by a simple observation: Romblon has more worth seeing than most itineraries ever reach, and the reason usually comes down to logistics, not lack of interest.

What followed was building the parts that actually matter for a multi-island province — real ferry and terminal data, offline-first design for weak inter-island signal, and route planning that treats crossings as the plan, not an inconvenience to work around.

The goal from day one has been the same: make the islands most visitors never reach easier to actually get to.

Where This Is Headed

A tool for the province, not just its visitors.

Beyond trip planning, Adventure is being developed with an eye toward supporting Romblon's tourism more broadly — in conversation with local government and community organizations who know the province best. If you represent a municipality, tourism office, or local organization in Romblon, we'd welcome the conversation.
